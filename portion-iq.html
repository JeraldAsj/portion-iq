The Real Reason Your Restaurant
Isn't As Profitable As It Should Be

It's NOT your sales volume. NOT your marketing. NOT your staff count. The answer is far more specific — and far more fixable.

  • 01
    Over-Portioning on Every PlateYour chefs give 'a bit extra' thinking it's generous. At 300 covers/day, that generosity is costing you thousands monthly.
  • 02
    Incorrect Yield AssumptionsYou price a dish based on raw ingredient cost. But after trimming, cooking, and plating, your actual yield may be 30–40% less.
  • 03
    Invisible Wastage Every ShiftSpoilage, over-trimming, burnt batches, dropped items — these don't appear on any report. They just vanish from your margin.

    Prep Over-Producing DailyExcess prep that doesn't sell becomes waste. Without production planning, you're throwing profit in the bin every night.

    Wrong Menu PricingYour menu prices weren't set using real costed recipes. They were guessed — or copied from competitors. You may be selling at a loss.
  • 06
    Inventory MismatchesWhat's on paper vs. what's on the shelf never matches. That gap represents real money — stolen, wasted, or miscounted.
  • 07
    Chef DependencyWhen your head chef calls in sick, consistency collapses. Your food cost spikes and quality drops — because it's all in one person's head.
